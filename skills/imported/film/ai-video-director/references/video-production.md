# Actual AI video production

Apply this workflow when the user asks for playable video. Match the scope and permissions to the brief, and stop at a truthful intermediate result when necessary tools are unavailable.

1. Identify current script or brief, approved reference assets, visual and creative locks, final format, cost limits, permissions and the requested delivery. Separate known facts and assumptions. Make normal creative decisions when the user has delegated them; do not rewrite locked structure for convenience.
2. Use the shared shot and prompt process to plan the action, information and sound. Choose shot count and duration from the story, not a prescribed minimum. Keep world geography, identity, props and lighting continuous. Resolve whether generation should use one clip, individual shots or groups based on the actual platform and desired result; do not invent provider support.
3. Before paid calls or sending material to a third party, follow the user's actual authorization, privacy constraints and applicable cost boundary. Generate real motion using tools that are available. A storyboard, still slideshow, preview or prompt is not a finished film.
4. Review available takes for performance, identity, readable action, composition, continuity and editability. Repair or regenerate only the part that needs it when possible. Do not claim to have inspected media that the host cannot view.
5. Assemble with suitable editing tools, preserving approved story and timing. Use meaningful cuts or handoffs through action, eyeline, occlusion, sound, information or reaction. If Video Editor is available, hand it the source material and locked creative plan; respect its authority over actual editorial timing, transcripts and captions. Add sound, dialogue, ambience, effects, silence and authorized music as appropriate; do not invent permission to add music, voiceover or captions.
6. Render a playable final file. When supported, watch the entire actual render at normal speed for story and rhythm and inspect it for continuity and artifacts; listen to the sound and check sync. Repair material issues and review again. A few still frames or technical metadata do not establish full playback review. If visual or audio review is unavailable, say so.
7. Return the actual video, format, duration and what was verified. Distinguish a reviewed final, a playable candidate awaiting aesthetic approval, and incomplete or unverified work. Never state the user accepted it unless they actually did. Disclose remaining material issues, missing capabilities and costs.
