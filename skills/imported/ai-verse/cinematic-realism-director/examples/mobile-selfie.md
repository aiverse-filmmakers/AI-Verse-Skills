# Example - Professional Mobile / Selfie AUTO

## User

```text
iPhone mirror selfie in a hotel room
```

## Expected Direction

```text
workflow = AUTO DIRECT
requested medium = mobile / iPhone / mirror selfie
professional specialty = elite mobile photographer + editor
question required = no
execution = native/local image model first
```

Professionalization must preserve the phone/selfie medium.

AUTO should infer:

- believable phone distance and mirror geometry;
- natural handheld/social framing rather than cinema blocking;
- best available room/window/practical-light position;
- controlled exposure and white balance;
- realistic skin with restrained phone processing;
- clean background hierarchy and mirror/reflection coherence;
- professional mobile color/local-contrast edit;
- subtle natural texture where compatible;
- no fake HDR, beauty-filter plasticity, impossible portrait-mode cutout, or cinema-lens bokeh.

## Example Prompt

```text
An iPhone mirror selfie in a hotel room, unmistakably photographed on a phone rather than a cinema camera. Natural handheld mirror composition with believable phone-to-mirror distance, realistic wide phone perspective and accurate reflection geometry. Use the most flattering plausible room/window light without turning the scene into a studio setup. Keep skin real and dimensional, highlights controlled, white balance clean, shadows natural, and the hotel-room background organized but believable.

Apply elite mobile-photo editing: restrained local contrast, clean color separation, natural skin tone, controlled phone sharpening and HDR behavior, subtle organic photographic texture if it improves naturalness. No beauty-filter skin, fake depth cutout, cinema-camera bokeh, anamorphic flare, teal-orange grade or heavy film grain.
```

## Pass Condition

The result should look like an exceptional iPhone photographer/editor made the image—not like a cinema-camera image with an iPhone mentioned in the prompt.
