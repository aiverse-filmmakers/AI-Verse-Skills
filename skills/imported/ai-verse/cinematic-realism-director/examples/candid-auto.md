# Example - Professional Candid AUTO

## User

```text
candid photo of two friends laughing outside a restaurant
```

## Expected Direction

```text
workflow = AUTO DIRECT
requested medium = candid / observational photography
professional specialty = elite candid / editorial / documentary photographer
question required = no
execution = native/local image model first
```

AUTO should prioritize:

- a decisive believable instant rather than a staged pose;
- natural laughter/body language;
- plausible photographer access/distance;
- layered restaurant/street context;
- composition that feels discovered but expertly judged;
- motivated available/practical light;
- professional exposure and color finishing;
- real skin, clothing and environmental materials;
- subtle organic photographic texture;
- no obvious beauty-light setup, staged symmetry, cinematic fog or decorative flare.

## Example Prompt

```text
A candid photograph of two friends laughing outside a restaurant, captured at a genuinely unposed decisive instant. The composition feels observational and discovered rather than arranged, but is judged with top editorial/documentary precision: natural body language, believable photographer distance, layered foreground/background context, and enough restaurant/street detail to place the moment.

Use only plausible available/practical light from the restaurant and street environment, with professional exposure control, natural skin tones, believable highlight rolloff and readable shadows. Preserve authentic clothing folds, glass/metal reflections, pavement contact and environment interaction. Finish with restrained editorial color, natural microcontrast and subtle fine organic photographic texture so the image feels photographed rather than synthetically perfect.

No fashion posing, obvious studio key, dramatic rim light, teal-orange grade, cinematic haze, anamorphic flare or exaggerated shallow depth unless the scene itself motivates it.
```

## Pass Condition

The image should feel like a top candid/editorial photographer captured an exceptional real moment, not like a staged film still pretending to be candid.
