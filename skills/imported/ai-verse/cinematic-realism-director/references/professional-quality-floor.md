# Professional Quality Floor

Status: RUNTIME POLICY

Purpose: guarantee that minimal user input still receives the strongest professional photographic execution appropriate to the requested medium, subject, and context.

## Core Promise

A user should not need to type `professional`, `cinematic`, `Hollywood`, `ARRI`, `good lighting`, `good composition`, `realistic`, or `high quality` to receive professional-quality direction.

Unless the user explicitly requests a rough, amateur, accidental, unedited, surveillance, disposable, low-quality, or otherwise degraded result, AUTO must apply a professional quality floor.

```text
basic request
-> infer the intended photographic medium
-> identify the best professional practice for that medium
-> elevate composition, capture, light, exposure, color, texture, materials, and finishing
-> preserve the user's literal subject/action/environment
```

The quality floor is not a fixed visual preset. It is a minimum standard of professional execution.

## Professionalization Is Medium-Aware

Do not turn every request into the same cinema-camera image.

The correct question is:

> If an elite professional specializing in this exact kind of image created it, what would they do while preserving the user's requested medium and moment?

### Mobile / iPhone / selfie / social photography

If the user asks for an iPhone photo, phone selfie, mirror selfie, casual phone picture, or similar mobile capture:

- preserve recognizable mobile-camera perspective and proximity;
- preserve the social/candid plausibility of the shot;
- use excellent subject placement, timing, available-light choice, exposure, skin rendering, white balance, local contrast, highlight control, and edit discipline;
- avoid converting the capture into an obvious cinema-camera frame unless the user asks for that;
- make it look like the work of an elite mobile photographer/editor, not a random snapshot;
- avoid beauty-filter plasticity, fake HDR, oversharpening, excessive portrait-mode cutout, or implausible lens blur.

Professional mobile does not mean sterile. Subtle organic texture may remain where it improves photographic naturalness.

### Candid / documentary / street photography

For candid, observational, documentary, reportage, or street moments:

- prioritize timing, believable access, natural body language, context, layering, gesture, and environmental light;
- composition should feel discovered rather than over-staged while still being exceptionally judged;
- use exposure and color finishing at the level of top editorial/documentary photography;
- preserve imperfections that make the moment believable;
- avoid fashion-pose behavior, excessive beauty light, theatrical haze, or obvious artificial staging unless requested.

### Narrative / cinematic still

For narrative, dramatic, movie-like, commercial-film, music-video, or otherwise cinematic photographic scenes:

- target feature-film-level shot design;
- use premium cinema-camera tonal behavior as an observable aesthetic target, with ARRI-like highlight rolloff and gentle highlight-to-mid transition where compatible;
- maintain rich but controlled shadow density;
- use professional color separation, believable skin, motivated light, intentional depth, and coherent lens behavior;
- preserve production-design and location information needed by the story;
- finish with subtle organic filmic texture by default unless the user requests pristine digital cleanliness.

`ARRI-like` describes observable tonal behavior. It does not claim literal ARRI capture when another model/provider creates the image.

### Portrait / beauty

For portraits:

- prioritize identity, expression, gaze, skin tone, facial dimensionality, posture, and background relationship;
- use flattering but physically credible light appropriate to the concept;
- retain real skin structure without pore overlays or plastic smoothing;
- apply professional retouching restraint;
- do not default to 85mm, f/1.4, beauty dish, or glamour styling unless appropriate.

### Fashion / editorial

For fashion/editorial:

- prioritize silhouette, garment behavior, pose, styling, location, attitude, fabric physics, and visual identity;
- allow bolder composition/optics when conceptually justified;
- maintain believable anatomy, textiles, light, and spatial relationships;
- finish to top editorial campaign standards rather than generic AI-fashion gloss.

### Product / commercial

For product imagery:

- prioritize exact product geometry, logos/text where required, material response, scale, controlled reflections, edge definition, contact, and brand-appropriate polish;
- use world-class commercial lighting and finishing appropriate to the product category;
- do not force film grain when a pristine packshot, beauty macro, clinical cosmetic shot, catalog requirement, or user instruction clearly calls for clean capture;
- environmental/product campaigns may still use subtle organic texture when compatible.

### Automotive

For automotive imagery:

- preserve body proportions, wheel geometry, paint response, reflections, road contact, environment, and motion state;
- use specialist automotive composition and surface-lighting logic;
- avoid wide-lens deformation that changes recognizable body design unless intentionally requested;
- maintain premium campaign-level finishing.

### Food

For food:

- prioritize edible material cues, moisture, crust, translucency, steam/condensation only when physically justified, plate geometry, serving action, and tactile detail;
- use specialist food-lighting and color discipline;
- avoid plastic gloss, floating garnish, uniform microcontrast, or oversaturation.

### Architecture / interiors

For architecture:

- prioritize camera position, vertical/horizon logic, spatial clarity, material scale, human scale, natural/artificial source balance, and controlled geometry;
- use elite architectural-photography discipline before any cinematic stylization;
- do not sacrifice geometry to shallow depth, fisheye distortion, or spectacle unless explicitly desired.

### Travel / hospitality

For travel/hospitality:

- balance destination identity, atmosphere, human experience, spatial legibility, material appeal, and aspirational polish;
- make the result look like the work of a top travel/hospitality photographer or cinematographer depending on the requested image type;
- avoid generic postcard perfection that erases lived experience.

## Default Photographic Finish

For normal photographic outputs, AUTO should usually include a restrained professional finish even when the user does not ask for one.

Typical baseline:

```text
intentional composition
professional exposure hierarchy
controlled highlight rolloff
natural shadow density
clean color separation
credible skin/material response
realistic lens/focus transition
non-brittle detail
subtle organic texture
professional color finishing
physical realism
```

This is the floor, not the ceiling.

## Default Cinema DNA

When the requested medium is cinematic/narrative or the request is a general photographic scene without a stronger medium signal, AUTO should normally target:

- feature-film-quality visual hierarchy;
- premium digital-cinema tonal response;
- soft, controlled highlight rolloff rather than harsh clipping or fake HDR;
- ARRI-like highlight and skin-tone behavior as an observable target where compatible;
- rich but readable shadows;
- motivated lighting with believable source direction and falloff;
- controlled color density and separation;
- realistic focus transition and lens depth;
- high-end skin/material rendering;
- subtle organic filmic texture;
- a polished grade that does not announce itself as a preset.

Do not interpret this as permission for automatic anamorphic flares, orange/teal, haze, rim light, maximum blur, lifted blacks, crushed shadows, or blockbuster color clichés.

## Grain / Filmic Texture Default

For most photographic and cinematic outputs, subtle organic filmic texture is a default finishing layer.

The target is:

```text
fine
restrained
non-uniform
luminance-aware
compatible with the capture medium
barely-to-gently visible at normal viewing size
```

Its purpose is to prevent sterile AI cleanliness and support a finished photographic image, not to advertise `film`.

### Normally keep subtle texture

- narrative/cinematic frames;
- candid/documentary/editorial photography;
- portraits;
- fashion;
- travel/hospitality;
- lifestyle/commercial scenes;
- mobile photography when it improves naturalness without breaking phone realism.

### Reduce or remove by default when the medium clearly requires pristine cleanliness

- clinical/medical imagery;
- technical documentation;
- e-commerce packshots requiring perfectly clean backgrounds;
- UI/screens/screenshots;
- diagrams/graphics outside this skill's normal scope;
- highly polished beauty/product macros where grain damages required material clarity;
- explicit `no grain`, `pristine digital`, `clinical`, `sterile`, `noise-free`, or equivalent instructions.

Strong/coarse grain still requires a specific reason.

## Professional Finish Is Not Cliché Stacking

The quality floor is compatible with anti-cliché discipline.

Default professional quality may include:

- excellent composition;
- refined exposure;
- premium highlight rolloff;
- natural skin/material response;
- professional color separation;
- subtle filmic texture;
- realistic optical falloff;
- polished grading.

It does **not** automatically include:

- anamorphic blue streaks;
- teal/orange grading;
- haze/fog;
- rim light;
- f/1.2 depth;
- Dutch angle;
- heavy bloom;
- obvious halation;
- dramatic vignette;
- underexposure;
- exaggerated chromatic aberration.

## User Locks and Explicit Aesthetic Overrides

The professional floor applies to unspecified decisions only.

Explicit user instructions override it.

Examples:

- `raw unedited phone snapshot` -> do not apply polished commercial finishing;
- `no grain` -> no grain;
- `clinical clean product image` -> prioritize pristine digital clarity;
- `harsh amateur flash` -> preserve amateur-flash character while still maintaining physical coherence;
- `security camera` -> preserve surveillance medium;
- `2007 compact-camera look` -> preserve that medium instead of forcing ARRI behavior.

Professional direction means executing the requested aesthetic expertly, not erasing it.

## AUTO Acceptance

AUTO fails this policy if:

- a sufficient one-line prompt results in merely competent/ordinary photography when stronger professional execution was safely inferable;
- the user must add `professional`, `cinematic`, `Hollywood`, `ARRI`, or `high quality` to unlock premium image direction;
- an iPhone/selfie request is turned into generic cinema-camera imagery rather than elite mobile photography;
- cinematic/narrative work lacks professional tonal response, color, light, depth, and finishing without a reason;
- a normal photographic image is left sterile/AI-clean when subtle organic texture would improve it;
- cliché avoidance is used as an excuse to remove professional finishing;
- the quality floor overrides an explicit user lock or requested degraded medium.
