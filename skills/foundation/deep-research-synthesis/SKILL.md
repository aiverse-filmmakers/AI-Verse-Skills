# Deep Research Synthesis

## Procedure
1. Define the decision/question, freshness requirement, scope, and evidence standard.
2. Prefer primary and authoritative evidence, then complementary secondary evidence.
3. Gather independent sources and record dates, claims, and limitations.
4. Reconcile conflicts by comparing definitions, periods, methods, incentives, and source quality.
5. Synthesize conclusions, uncertainty, dissenting evidence, and what could change the conclusion.

## Constraints
- Material claims require provenance.
- Inference is labeled separately from sourced fact.
- Conflicting evidence is surfaced, not silently averaged.

## Verification
Freshness matches the question and the conclusion can be traced back to the evidence used.
